How to read it

joints · bones · fingertips

Each rule in your rubric is compared with what was measured. A miss becomes a reason with its moment, and the video goes to a reviewer.

Two sides of the same delivery.

The collector films and delivers. The lab sets what counts as good. Ormes applies the same rubric to every video, so both sides read the same result.

For collectors

  • Check before you deliver. Contributors get the reason and a hint within seconds, so they refilm instead of waiting days.
  • Only doubtful videos reach your reviewers. The rest is decided with a reason you can show.
  • Your rubric comes from the lab’s spec. Every save is a new version, and each decision records the one it used.

For labs

  • You set the spec. The lab writes or approves the rubric, so “good” means the same thing for every collector.
  • Every delivery comes with the same report. Measurements per video and the rubric version used.
  • The record stays, the footage does not. Videos are deleted after analysis; decisions and measurements are kept.
