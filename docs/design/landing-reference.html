What Quellix keeps

  • The decision and its reasons.
  • The measurement behind each check.
  • The rubric version that was used.
  • A fingerprint that can’t be turned back into video.

What it doesn’t

  • The video, after the analysis.
  • Faces, documents and screens it flagged.
  • Contributor names and payment details.
  • Anything visible to other customers.
  • Training data for any model.

What Quellix doesn’t do.

It doesn’t sell your data.

We check your videos and delete them. We don’t keep them and we don’t offer them to labs.

It doesn’t take the last word.

You set the rubric, your reviewers can override any decision, and contributors can contest a rejection with a person.

It doesn’t promise what your lab will accept.

It applies the spec you give it, the same way on every video. Upload your lab’s decisions and we measure ourselves against them.

First-person video comes first.

We start with egocentric video from phones and action cameras. Robot data and other sensors come later.

Questions

Do I have to rebuild my app?

No. Call the API where you approve videos today. Tasks, payouts and customers stay where they are.

Who sets the criteria?

You, usually from your buyer’s spec. Each task has its own rubric, and every decision records the rubric version it used.

How accurate is it?

We report precision and recall on rejections, compared with your own acceptance rate, on videos your reviewers or your lab have already judged. Doubtful cases go to review instead of being rejected.

Can I measure it against my lab’s decisions?

Yes. Upload the lab’s accept or reject outcomes with your videos, and the report compares our decisions with theirs.

Does Quellix keep my videos?

No. Videos are deleted after the analysis and never used to train models. We list every provider that processes them, and a DPA is available for every customer.

What do I get back for each video?

The decision, the reasons and each measurement: when the task starts and ends, how long hands are visible, blur, privacy flags, originality, and the rubric version.

Every video you pay for teaches a robot.

Automated acceptance for companies that pay people to film their work. Upload videos your reviewers have already judged and see the report.
