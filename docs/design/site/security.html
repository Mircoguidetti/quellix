Security and data

Videos are checked, not kept.

What happens to a video from upload to deletion, what we keep, and who processes it.

The life of a video

  1. Upload

    Private storage

    The file goes to a private bucket through a signed URL that expires. No public link exists.

  2. Analysis

    Checked once

    The worker downloads it, runs the checks and writes the decision.

  3. Decision

    Deleted

    Accepted and rejected videos are deleted right after the decision. Failed analyses too, after the last retry.

  4. Review

    14 days at most

    Videos waiting for a reviewer are kept until a person decides, and never longer than 14 days.

What we keep, and what we don’t.

Kept, per customer

  • Decisions, reasons and every measurement
  • The rubric version each decision used
  • Reviewer decisions and notes
  • Your lab’s outcomes
  • Fingerprints that can’t be turned back into video

Not kept

  • The video, after the analysis
  • Contributor names, emails or payment details: only your opaque id
  • Anything visible to another customer

Models and training

No training without opt-in.

Your videos train nothing unless you turn on training in Settings. If you do, they train Quellix’s own models only, and you pay less.

No third-party models by default.

Customer video is never sent to an external model provider unless your organisation enables it. It is off by default.

Never resold.

We don’t sell, share or offer your videos or measurements to labs or anyone else.

A DPA for everyone.

A data processing agreement is included for every customer.

Isolation and keys

Per-organisation access

Every table is protected by row-level security on your organisation. Duplicate matching never looks across customers.

Keys stored as hashes

API keys and webhook secrets are shown once. We keep only a hash of each API key.

Signed webhooks

HMAC-SHA256 with a timestamp, so you can reject forged or replayed requests.
